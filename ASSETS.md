### Tier 3 — Original Custom Artwork & Comic-Book Vectors
All character motifs and multiverse elements in this proposal are created as original geometric compositions, abstract silhouettes, and dimensional portal effects to prevent trademark infringement or likeness misuse.
- **Doctor Strange Dimensional Portal**: Original SVG radial mandala with mystic sparks, rotating eldritch geometry, and ember particles.
- **Iron Man Arc Silhouette**: Original high-contrast silhouette with glowing triangular arc reactor and repulsor energy vectors.
- **Spider-Man Web Multiverse Lattice**: Original dynamic diagonal comic-panel divider with geometric web tessellations.
- **Thor Asgardian Lightning / Bifrost**: Original electric energy discharge vectors and chromatic panel split.
- **Captain America Vibranium Shield Lockup**: Concentric vector star badge used as issue number / chapter emblem.
- **Black Panther Vibranium Weave**: Geometric African-futurist kinetic violet mesh background pattern.
- **Infinity Stones Motif**: Hexagonal energy node system representing the 6 cosmic disciplines of Vishwakarandak (Space/Exploration, Reality/Culture, Power/Sports, Mind/Technical, Time/Timeline, Soul/Council).
